About the gym

Twenty-two years on the forest road

Rainbow Gym & Fitness Spa opened in Chinnamanur in 2003, on the quiet stretch of Forest Office Road near West Mullaivanam — one of the last streets before Megamalai's tea and forest country begins. In 2022 the whole space was rebuilt with new, top-tier strength and cardio equipment, without losing the neighbourhood gym feel that's kept members coming back for two decades.

Today it runs as both a training floor and a recovery space — gym on one side, spa on the other — with online coaching for members who can't always make it in.
